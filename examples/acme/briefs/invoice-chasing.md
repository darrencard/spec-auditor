# Invoice chasing

Illustrative sample brief for Acme, an invented company.

## Landing brief

- **Problem:** Small trades businesses wait weeks to be paid because nobody has time to chase.
- **Metric:** Days sales outstanding
- **Bar:** 30
- **Better:** lower
- **By:** 2026-12-31
- **Non-goals:** No debt collection. No late fees.
- **Tokens per task:** 4,000
- **Price per token:** $0.000003
- **The line:** model call. The chaser drafts the message; who gets chased and when stays in fixed code.
- **Decides the next step:** Head of Product, Payments squad
