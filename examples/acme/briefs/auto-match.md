# Auto match

Illustrative sample brief for Acme, an invented company.

## Landing brief

- **Problem:** Bookkeepers match bank lines to invoices by hand every week.
- **Metric:** Invoice auto-match rate
- **Bar:** 80%
- **Better:** higher
- **By:** 2026-12-31
- **Non-goals:** No bank feeds beyond the current three.
- **Tokens per task:** 2,000
- **Price per token:** $0.000003
- **The line:** model call. Fuzzy matching of payee names calls a model; exact matches stay in fixed code.
- **Decides the next step:** Head of Product, Ledger squad
